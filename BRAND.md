# Zoomies: brand and product profile

The fictional business behind the Zoomies site. It exists so the design has a real
concept to work against. Everything here is invented. Cats remain untrackable by
design.

## One-liner

**Zoomies is Strava for cats.** A featherweight smart collar logs every sprint,
stalk, and sunbeam nap, then turns it into stats your cat will pretend not to care
about.

## What it is

A wearable activity tracker (the **Zoomies Collar**) plus a companion app. A 9-axis
sensor and on-device ML auto-detect activities: zoomies (sprints), hunts (stalking
and pouncing), naps (with sleep stages), and grooming. The app surfaces it as
activity rings, heatmaps, leaderboards, insights, and alerts.

## Mission

Help people understand and bond with their cats through playful, precise activity
data. Take cat behavior as seriously (and as unseriously) as athletes take their own.

## Positioning

The quantified-self category (Whoop, Oura, Strava, Apple Fitness) pointed at the
most committed athlete in the home: the cat. Premium hardware, real data science, and
a straight face about deeply silly source data.

## ICP (ideal customer profile)

- **Primary: the Quantified Cat Parent.** 25 to 40, urban or suburban, data-curious,
  already wears an Apple Watch / Oura / Whoop. Loves their cat intensely and a little
  ironically. Active in cat-internet (TikTok, Instagram). Has disposable income for a
  premium pet gadget. Wants three things: connection (what does my cat do all day),
  reassurance (is my cat healthy and normal), and bragging rights.
- **Secondary: multi-cat households.** The leaderboard and household competition are
  the hook. One subscription, many athletes.
- **Tertiary: vets and clinics.** Activity history and anomaly detection as a clinical
  signal (sudden lethargy or hyperactivity).
- **Jobs to be done:** understand my cat's day while I am at work; get an early warning
  if something is off; have fun and share it; feel closer to an animal that ignores me.

## Brand personality and voice

Playful but premium. Dry, deadpan, data-loving. We treat absurd cat behavior with the
seriousness of performance analytics, and let the gap do the joke.

- **Tone words:** witty, precise, warm, deadpan, energetic but restrained.
- **Do:** lead with a real-sounding metric, then undercut it gently with cat reality
  ("professional resting," "appropriately concerned from bed"). Use confident,
  tabular numbers. Be specific.
- **Do not:** baby-talk, "fur babies," exclamation spam, or clip art. The humor comes
  from the deadpan, not from being cute.
- **Voice example:** "Mochi logged 7 zoomies today, peaking at 19.4 mph at 3:02am. We
  have notified no one, because you were already awake."

## Visual identity

- **Logo:** the wordmark **Zoomies** in a confident geometric sans, paired with a
  paw mark. The mark can carry a subtle motion streak to imply speed. See the inline
  SVG paw in `index.html` and the activity-ring motif.
- **Color (meaning, not decoration):**
  - Base: warm near-black `#0b0b0d`, off-white text `#f3f3f5`.
  - Brand accent: **Zoomie Lime `#c2f04a`** (electric, athletic, distinctive, and
    deliberately not the generic indigo/cyan/magenta gradient palette).
  - Category hues, each meaning one thing: **lime = zoomies**, **blue `#6ea8ff` =
    rest/sleep**, **amber `#ffb24a` = hunt**.
- **Type:** clean grotesque sans, large editorial display sizes, and **tabular
  numerals** everywhere data appears (the athletic-data feel).
- **Motifs:** activity rings, heatmap grids, motion streaks, paw marks, data tiles,
  grain over dark surfaces. Restraint over decoration: the product visuals carry the
  page, not a background gradient.

## Product features

1. **Auto-detected activities.** 9-axis sensor plus on-device ML classifies zoomies,
   hunts, naps, and grooming. No taps.
2. **Activity rings.** Daily Zoomies, Hunt, and Rest goals that close as the day goes.
3. **Zoomies heatmap.** Intensity by hour and day. The 3am pattern is real and now
   provable.
4. **Nap stage analysis.** Light, deep, and full loaf, with daily totals.
5. **3am zoomie alerts.** Real-time push the instant a sprint starts.
6. **Leaderboards.** Neighborhood, breed, and household rankings by weekly zoomie score.
7. **Health insights.** Anomaly detection flags sudden lethargy or hyperactivity and
   nudges a vet visit.
8. **Performance stats.** Top speed, distance, and a "treats burned" calorie estimate.
9. **Multi-cat and vet sharing.** Per-cat profiles and exportable history.

## Hardware

Featherweight, waterproof Zoomies Collar. 30-day battery. Breakaway safety clasp.
Comes in three colors (Midnight, Sand, Zoomie Lime).

## Pricing (flavor)

- **Collar:** $99 one-time.
- **Free:** rings and basic daily stats.
- **Zoomies+:** $9/mo for full history, heatmaps, insights, and anomaly alerts.
